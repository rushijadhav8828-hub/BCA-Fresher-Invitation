## 🌟 Highlights & Features
- 👑 **Hierarchical Multi-Profile Switcher:** Ordered by Principal, Vice Principal, HOD, Coordinator, and Faculty.
- 📋 **One-Click Share & Copy Link:** Copy the direct personalized link for any dignitary or teacher, or share directly to WhatsApp with prefilled respectful invitation message.
- 💌 **Interactive Royal Wax Envelope:** Unsealing ceremony with fanfare and golden confetti.
- 🔥 **"IGNITE - 2026" Flame Aesthetics:** Fiery gradients, shimmering ember effects, and festive typography.
- 🎆 **Particle & Confetti Systems:** 60fps golden ember canvas and dynamic fireworks confetti cannon.
- 🎵 **Zero-Dependency Synthesizer Audio:** Web Audio API party chord progression and fanfare sounds.
- ⏳ **Live Countdown Timer:** Real-time ticker counting down to 14 October 2026, 11:30 AM.
- 🖨️ **Print & PDF Support:** High-resolution physical invitation card stylesheet for printing or framing.
